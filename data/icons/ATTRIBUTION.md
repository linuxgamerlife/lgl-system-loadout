## Additional application icons

The remaining application icons in this directory were copied from the
installed hicolor, Papirus, Breeze, Slot-Spectrum and Shadow icon themes, or
from Flathub, so the cards show the real application artwork before that
application is installed. They remain the property of their respective
projects and are used only to identify those applications. Their licences
follow the source theme or application; see the upstream projects for the
full terms. This includes the Nemo icon, which comes from the Slot-Spectrum
theme.
